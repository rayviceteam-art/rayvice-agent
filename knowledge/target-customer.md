# Target Customer — ICP (Australia only)

**Positive signals:** Australia + independent/sole trader + NDIS support work + billing/admin pain (invoice, spreadsheet, paperwork, payment problems) + small operator.
**Negative signals:** large enterprise, outside Australia, unrelated industry, spam/fake/duplicate.
**Never infer:** disability, medical status, politics, religion, ethnicity, sexuality. Only business-relevant info.
**Do NOT target:** random global SaaS users, large enterprises, unrelated agencies, generic freelancers.
